Frequently asked questions

What runtimes do you support?
We natively support Node.js, Python, Go, and Rust. You can also run any WASM-compatible language. All runtimes execute in our secure V8-based isolation environment.
How does pricing work?
Pricing is based on execution duration (GB-seconds) and total requests. You get 100k requests free per month. You don't pay for idle time.
What is the maximum execution time?
Default execution time is 10 seconds for HTTP requests, but background jobs can run for up to 15 minutes on the Pro plan.

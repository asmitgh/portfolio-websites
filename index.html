Common Questions

Does the WAF introduce latency?
Our security inspections happen at the edge in under 2ms. Because we process requests close to the user, total round-trip time is often faster than sending unoptimized traffic to your origin.
Is SSL/TLS included?
Yes. We automatically provision and renew wildcard SSL certificates for all your domains. We support TLS 1.3 and HSTS by default.
Can I export logs to SIEM?
Enterprise plans support real-time log streaming to Datadog, Splunk, S3, or any HTTP endpoint for integration with your existing security operations.

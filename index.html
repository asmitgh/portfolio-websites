Frequently asked questions

What frameworks do you support?
We support virtually all modern frontend frameworks including Next.js, React, Vue, Nuxt, SvelteKit, Astro, and plain static HTML/CSS sites. Our build system automatically detects your framework and configures the optimal settings.
How does the pricing work?
We offer a generous free tier for hobbyists. Pro plans start at $20/month per seat, and usage is billed based on bandwidth and build minutes. You can set spend limits to ensure you never go over budget.
Do I need to configure the CDN?
No. Every deployment is automatically pushed to our global edge network. Assets are cached and served from the location nearest to your user, with instant cache invalidation on new deploys.
